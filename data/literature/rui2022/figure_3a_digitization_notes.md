# Rui 2022 main-text Figure 3A digitization notes

Source: Rui et al., Macromolecules 2022, 55, 9705-9714, Figure 3A.

The source PDF page containing Figure 3A was rendered at 600 dpi. Plot-axis calibration used the printed limits `T = -40..50 C` and `epsilon_c = 11..19`; filled red-circle and blue-square marker cores were isolated from their connecting lines by color thresholding and morphological opening. Marker centroids were converted to data coordinates and rounded to three decimals for the canonical CSV.

Canonical data file:

`data/literature/rui2022/figure_3a_film_eps_digitized.csv`

Estimated reading uncertainty is small relative to the v0.1.8 film-closure discrepancy; the project does not use the third decimal place as an independently measured precision claim.
